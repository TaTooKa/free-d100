# Character Creation

Follow these guidelines to create characters in the *Free d100* system.

## Skills

The GM decides the "level of expertise" of the player characters. The typical is to have somewhat-competent protagonists, so the classic array is as follows:

**One** Skill at *Expert* (70%), **Two** at *Trained* (60%), **Three** at *Dabbler* (40%), **Four** at *Enthusiast* (30%).

Each player builds their character skillset with these 10 values, choosing from the skills available for the setting or scenario to be played. The rest of their character skills will remain at their default values (20% if *Instinctive*, 0% if *Specialized*; see [Skills](/skills))

## Gear

Each specific setting or scenario will have special rules on how to acquire starting Gear for new characters. Some will require to spend money to "buy" items, while others will just grant specific tools, weapons or armor for each archetype, class or profession listed.

## Other Details

Each specific setting or scenario will have rules and procedures on how to generate extra details, flavor and descriptors for characters, either by choosing from lists or by rolling in random tables.
