# Free d100

Free d100 is a universal d100-based TTRPG system. It is shared as a web-page, for free (as in *freedom*, and as in *free beer*), under a [CC0 license](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/deed.en) (it is automatically in the *public domain*).

TTRPGs are more of an art form or a cultural activity than a product or IP to make money off. Their rules,  mechanics, and everything else should be forever free to tinker, modify, hack and share with others. Do with this whatever you want.
