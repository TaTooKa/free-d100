# Free d100

Free d100 is a universal d100-based Tabletop Roleplaying Game system. It is shared as a web-page, for free (as in *freedom*, and as in *free beer*), under a [CC0 license](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/deed.en) (it is automatically in the *public domain*).

TTRPGs are more of an art form or a cultural activity than a product or IP to make money off. Their rules,  mechanics, and everything else should be forever free to tinker, modify, hack and share with others. Do with this whatever you want.  

## Manifesto

**The objective** of a *Tabletop Roleplaying Game* is to **Roleplay** characters that have agency in a shared, imagined world. This might produce a "narrative" of the events (that can be retold after the fact), but during play, the game should mostly resemble a real-time, living simulation.

**The Game-Master** is an impartial arbiter that controls the universe and its inhabitants, and provides situations and obstacles without expecting a specific resolution or course of action (that is for the player characters to decide). The universe should be a *Sandbox* that reacts to the player characters' actions, but it still behaves like a living world even without their intervention. The GM must operate within the established *Intent* and *Theme* of the scenario, and if changing it, say so explicitly to adjust everyone's expectations.

**The Players** must create characters that are aligned with the *Intent* and *Theme* of the scenario as established by the GM. Each player must Roleplay honestly and strive to think and act *in-character* as much as possible. The GM and the game should provide them the *Tactical Infinity* required to be able to "try anything they can think of", but their actions should be limited not only by the context of the narration and the mechanics and probabilities of the game system, but also by their characters' beliefs, needs, fears and taboos. Each player should be mindful of the *Boundaries* and *play culture* established at the table.

**The Game** requires a *high-trust* environment, in which players and GM can lean on each other, knowing that their actions (both as players and *in-character*) are honest and strive to comply with the aforementioned tenets. Alignment on desired **Intent** (what is expected for the characters to do in the story), **Theme** (expected genre and desired/undesired tropes and clichés) and **Boundaries** (established topics that are out of bounds or that require extra-care when touched upon) is mandatory to have an enjoyable environment in which all participants can Roleplay and have fun.
