# Basic Rules

## Action Resolution

Roll d% equal or under the **Probability %** (but as high as possible to get more SLs).

The Probability % is usually determined by your **Skill %** (and other circumstantial ±% modifiers).

> [!NOTE]
> results between 00-99; Probability % can never be < 1% or > 97%.

## Success Levels
The tens in the dice result determine Success Levels (SLs) of the action. The higher, the better. SLs are used for determining "how good" an action result is (SL 0 just means a partial or meager success). The higher your Skill %, the higher the margin to obtain a better SL.
In Combat, SLs are added to weapon base damage to determine damage dealt.
