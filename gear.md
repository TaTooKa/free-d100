# Gear

This section covers certain rules and item listings for a modern/realistic setting.

## Gear Rules

### Carrying Capacity

Characters can carry up to 5 items without being hindered.

*Bulky* items take 2 slots. 5 "*small*" items (stackables, bits and bobs) take 1 slot.

Certain "*holders*" like bags, satchels, holsters, etc can increase the default limit.

### Overencumbered

* If a character exceeds their Carrying Capacity by 1 or 2, they have a hindering penalty of -20% to all rolls and move at ½ speed.

* If a character exceeds it by 3 or more, they cannot move at all and automatically fail all related checks.

## Weapons

**Base DMG**: The [DMG](/basic-rules#damage-dmg) to add to [SLs](/basic-rules#success-levels) obtained in the attack roll.

**Hands**: whether the weapon can be used one-handed, or two-handed. Note that every two-handed weapon is *Bulky*.

**Clip size** / **Ammo type**: determines how often you need to [Reload](/basic-rules#ammo-and-reloading) and which type of Ammo it uses.

**Effective Range**: each ranged weapon lists the [Range bands](/basic-rules#range) in which they are supposed to operate (**A**djacent, **P**oint-Blank, **S**hort, **M**edium, **L**ong, **E**xtreme, **B**eyond). Firing outside those Ranges imposes a **-30% penalty** for each increment. 

**Armor-Piercing Ammo**: ignores 1 point of [Armor](/basic-rules#armor); treats [Total Cover](/basic-rules#cover) as [Full Cover](/basic-rules#cover).

**Improvised firearms**: *Pipe Guns*, *Slam Guns*, etc, function the same as the actual model or type of that regular firearm, but as "*bad quality*".

**bad quality weapons**: DMG -1; **good quality weapons**: DMG +1.
