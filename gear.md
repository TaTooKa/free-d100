# Gear

This section covers certain rules and item listings for a modern/realistic setting.

## Gear Rules

### Carrying Capacity

Characters have 5 "Bulk Slots" in which they can carry up to 5 normal items without being hindered.

*Bulky* items take 2 slots. 5 "*small*" items (stackables, bits and bobs) take 1 slot.

Certain "*holders*" like bags, satchels, holsters, etc can increase the default limit.

### Encumbrance

* If a character exceeds their Carrying Capacity by 1 or 2, they are **Encumbered**: they have a hindering penalty of -20% to all rolls and move at ½ speed.

* If a character exceeds it by 3 or more, they are **Overencumbered**: they cannot move at all and automatically fail all related checks.

## Weapons

**Base DMG**: The [DMG](/basic-rules#damage-dmg) to add to [SLs](/basic-rules#success-levels) obtained in the attack roll.

**Hands**: whether the weapon can be used one-handed, or two-handed. Note that every two-handed weapon is *Bulky*.

**Clip size** / **Ammo type**: determines how often you need to [Reload](/basic-rules#ammo-and-reloading) and which type of Ammo it uses.

**Effective Range**: each ranged weapon lists the [Range bands](/basic-rules#range) in which they are supposed to operate (**A**djacent, **P**oint-Blank, **S**hort, **M**edium, **L**ong, **E**xtreme, **B**eyond). Firing outside those Ranges imposes a **-30% penalty** for each increment. 

**Armor-Piercing Ammo**: ignores 1 point of [Armor](/basic-rules#armor); treats [Total Cover](/basic-rules#cover) as [Full Cover](/basic-rules#cover).

**Improvised firearms**: *Pipe Guns*, *Slam Guns*, etc, function the same as the actual model or type of that regular firearm, but as "*bad quality*".

**bad quality weapons**: DMG -1; **good quality weapons**: DMG +1.

## Explosives

Explosives have different DMG values depending on [Range](/basic-rules#range). They deal the listed base DMG to anything in that Range.

All Explosive DMG counts as *Blunt* and *Fire/Corrosive* unless otherwise stated.

**Thrown** explosives (like grenades) use the *Throwing* skill; **Set** explosives (like a timed bomb) use the *Demolitions* skill.

> [!NOTE]
> Failing a *Throwing* roll means the GM chooses where the ordnance lands (closer to the attacker, way past the enemy, etc).
>
> Failing a *Demolitions* roll might mean the explosive doesn't go off, or activates prematurely or too late, or has unintended effects / collateral; at GM's discretion.

## Armor

Armor is used in pieces that protect different [Hit Locations](/basic-rules#hit-locations). Only one piece can be equipped at each location.

Each piece can be made of different materials that determine its Armor Rating:

* **Light armor** (1-2) is made of thick fabrics, leather, light plastics, soft wood, bone, etc.
* **Medium armor** (3-4) is made of hardened leather, strong plastics, thin metal plates, chainmail, etc.
* **Heavy armor** (5-6) is made of thick or reinforced metal plating.

Each piece of armor takes 1, 2 or 3 slots of [Carrying Capacity](#carrying-capacity), depending on its material/rating.

Examples of armor pieces by Location:

* **Legs**: Trousers, Greaves, Faulds, Boots
* **Arms**: Armguards, Vambraces, Gauntlets, Sleeves, Pauldrons, Spaulders
* **Chest**: Vests, Breastplates, Cuirasses, Bandoliers, Jackets
* **Head**: Helmet, Hat, Mask

**Bad Quality Armor**: -1 Armor.
