# Skills

Characters have a list of Skills, each with a rating %.

Skills can be anything, but are roughly split in two categories: *Instinctive* and *Specialized*. 

* *Instinctive* (Ⓘ) skills are those that basically everyone has access or training in, or have probably already used before in their lives (Running, Dodging, Lying, etc). These are assumed to be at a **minimum of 20%**. 

* *Specialized* (Ⓢ) skills are those that require academic or formal training (Surgery, Astrophysics, Military Strategy, etc). These are assumed to be at **0% by default**. 

> [!NOTE]
> (even with a Skill at 0% you can attempt an action; only a result of 00 would succeed). 

## Skill Level Thresholds
* Untrained: **20%** (*Instinctive* Skills) or **0%** (*Specialized* Skills) 
* Enthusiast: **30%** 
* Dabbler: **40%** 
* Trained: **60%** 
* Expert: **70%** 
* Veteran: **80%** 
* Master: **90%** 

## Increasing Skill %

Skills can improve naturally just by doing actions (up to a certain point), and can also be increased by spending time training.

### Natural Improvement

* From 0% to 29% (*Untrained* to *Enthusiast*), any skill can be improved **just by using it**: after rolling it, increase it by +1%.
* From 30% to 59% (*Enthusiast* to *Trained*), Only on a **Failure** or **Critical** Success/Failure, improve the Skill rolled by +1%.
* From 60% to 69% (*Trained* to *Expert*), only **Criticals** improve it, by +1%.
* From 70% onwards (*Expert* and up), you can only improve it by **Training**.

### Training

Training requires the character to spend downtime equal to the current Skill % in days, in which they practice, study, rehearse or work out several hours per day.

After the period passes, roll d100: 

* If equal or higher to the current Skill %, increase it by 5%.
* If lower than the current Skill %, increase it by 1%.
